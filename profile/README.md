# Adolfson & Peterson Construction

## WE BUILD.
### Trust. Communities. People. _And the software that helps them._

---

For 80 years, Adolfson & Peterson Construction has built the spaces where communities live, learn, heal, and work. From a basement in Richfield, Minnesota in 1946 to 12 offices and 900+ team members across the country today, our foundation has always been the same: **the power of people, and a substitute for nothing — except integrity.**

This GitHub organization is the home of **AP Technology Development** — the team building the internal software, automation, and AI tooling that supports the work AP does every day in the field, in the office, and on the job site.

We bring the same values to code that AP brings to construction.

## What we build here

The code in this organization powers the systems AP teammates rely on:

- 🏗️ **Project & Financial Management** — purpose-built applications that streamline how project managers, coordinators, and finance partners run construction work from preconstruction through closeout
- 🤖 **AI Agent Platform** — a growing fleet of specialized AI agents that assist with documentation, IT support, financial workflows, and knowledge management — built to amplify our people, not replace them
- 🔧 **Internal Developer Platform** — shared infrastructure, libraries, and automation that let a small team punch above its weight
- 📐 **Integrations & Workflow Automation** — the connective tissue between construction-industry systems (estimating, scheduling, document control, ERP) that keep our projects moving

> _Most of this work is private to AP. If you're an AP team member, your Tech Development contact can get you set up with access. If you're an industry partner or a candidate exploring what life looks like inside AP's Tech Development team, welcome._

## Featured initiatives

### Project Financial Management (PFM)
Our next-generation platform for running the financial side of a construction project end-to-end. Commitments, change events, partner directories, document automation, mobile-friendly workflows — built around a **"Workbench"** pattern that puts the business object at the center and surfaces only what each role needs to see.

### AI Agent Fleet
A small but growing team of named AI agents, each specialized for a role: backend engineering, UX, IT support, technical writing, and more. Each operates with its own identity, its own memory, and its own scope of work — built to amplify human teammates, not replace them.

### Knowledge Infrastructure
Decades of construction know-how and tens of thousands of IT support tickets, structured and searchable, so the right answer is one query away.
